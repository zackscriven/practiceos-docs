<Steps>
  <Step title="Check the connection">
    Go to **Settings → My Profile → Email and conversations**. Under **Email (2-way sync)**, your account should show **Connected**. If it doesn't, connect it again.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Finish both permission prompts">
    Connecting can take two passes. If you closed the second prompt, the connection didn't finish — start it again and complete both.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check who the email is from">
    Two-way sync only brings in email with people who are **already contacts**, and only from the day you connected. Older email, or email with people who aren't contacts, won't appear.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Automated emails landing in spam

That's usually the sending domain, not your inbox connection. See [Set up your sending domain](/communications/email-sending).

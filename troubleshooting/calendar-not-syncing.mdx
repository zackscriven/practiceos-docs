---
title: Calendar isn't syncing
description: What to check when events don't block your availability or bookings don't appear on your calendar.
status: drafted
source: UI checked in a demo account 2026-10-13
---

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check the calendar is connected">
    Go to **Settings → Calendars → Connections**. Your calendar should be listed under **Connected calendars**. If you see **No connections found**, connect it with **+ Add new**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check your schedule is applied">
    In **Settings → Calendars → Availability**, look at **Active on**. If it shows **0 calendars**, you'll see the warning *Apply this schedule to at least one calendar to use it* — choose your calendar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check your timezones match">
    Compare the timezone on your schedule with **Settings → Business Profile → Time Zone**. A mismatch shifts every booking time.
  </Step>
</Steps>

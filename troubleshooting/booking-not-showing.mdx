---
title: No times are showing for booking
description: What to check when your booking page shows no available times.
status: drafted
source: UI checked in a demo account 2026-10-13
---

<Steps>
  <Step title="Your schedule is applied to the calendar">
    In **Settings → Calendars → Availability**, **Active on** must include the booking calendar. A schedule active on **0 calendars** offers no times.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The day is ticked">
    Under **Weekly working hours**, make sure the days you want are ticked and have start and end times.
  </Step>
  <Step title="No date-specific block">
    Check **Date specific hours** for dates you marked unavailable and forgot to remove.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Your own calendar isn't full">
    Events on your connected calendar block those times. A long all-day event can hide a whole day.
  </Step>
</Steps>

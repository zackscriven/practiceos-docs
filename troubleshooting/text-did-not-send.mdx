<Steps>
  <Step title="Is your number registered?">
    US carriers block business texts from numbers that haven't completed **A2P 10DLC** registration. If registration is pending or was rejected, texts won't be delivered. See [Phone numbers and texting](/communications/phone-sms).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Is there a texting number?">
    Check **Settings → Phone System → Phone numbers** for an active number.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Is your wallet funded?">
    Texts are paid from your usage wallet. Check that a card is on file in **Settings → Billing**. See [Billing and your wallet](/administration/billing).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Has the person opted out?">
    If someone replied STOP, they won't receive texts until they opt back in.
    {/* VERIFY: where an opted-out (DND) status shows on the contact record. */}
  </Step>
</Steps>

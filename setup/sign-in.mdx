## Before you begin

- The sign-in email and temporary password you received when your account was created
- Your phone, with an authenticator app installed (for example Google Authenticator or Microsoft Authenticator)

## Sign in for the first time

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the sign-in page">
    Go to [my.privatepracticeos.app](https://my.privatepracticeos.app) and sign in with your email and temporary password.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Change your password">
    Open **Settings → My Profile → Security**. Enter your current password, then your new password twice, and click **Update password**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn on two-factor authentication">
    On the same **Security** tab, set up **Two-factor Authentication (2FA) App** with your authenticator app. Once it's on, sign-ins can be confirmed with the app as well as your phone number and email.
    {/* VERIFY: label of the button that starts 2FA setup on an account where it is not yet enabled. */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save a backup code">
    Click **Generate Backup Code** and keep the code somewhere safe, such as your password manager. You'll need it if you lose your phone.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
**Sign Out Everywhere**, also on the **Security** tab, signs you out of every device at once — useful if you've signed in on a computer that isn't yours.
</Tip>

## The Launchpad

**Launchpad**, at the top of the left-hand menu, is a setup checklist for your account. If you have an onboarding call scheduled, you'll work through it together.

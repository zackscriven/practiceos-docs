## The Launchpad

The first time you sign in you'll see the **Launchpad**, a setup checklist. It's a one-time screen — once you dismiss it, it doesn't come back.

<Warning>
If you have an onboarding call scheduled, leave the Launchpad open until then.
</Warning>

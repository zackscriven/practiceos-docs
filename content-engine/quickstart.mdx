You bring the expertise. We turn it into content that builds your brand, grows your audience, and attracts new clients. No editing, no scheduling posts. You show up and share what you know, and we handle the rest.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Complete your content onboarding">
    Tell us about your business, your ideal audience, and what you'd like to accomplish. It takes about 3 to 5 minutes. The form is linked from your **Start Here** guide in Whop.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Book your content session">
    [Book your content session](https://links.privatepracticeos.app/widget/booking/oy6tQhe4mBpy6NSwzOwW), a 60-minute recording with Zack Scriven. See [Prepare for your session](/content-engine/prepare-for-your-session).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect your social accounts">
    We help you give us access to your social channels through each platform's own tools, so we can publish for you. You never send us a password.
  </Step>
  <Step title="We create and distribute your content">
    After your recording, we edit, caption, schedule, and publish. See [After your session](/content-engine/after-your-session).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Join the private client community">
    Your membership includes our private clients-only Discord community, granted automatically through Whop.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The Content Engine is powered by PracticeOS, but this membership does not include direct access to the PracticeOS software. If you want the CRM, automations, scheduling, pipelines, and weekly group calls as well, see the [Growth System](/growth-system/quickstart).

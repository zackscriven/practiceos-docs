After your recording, our team handles production. We:

- Find the strongest moments in the conversation
- Edit engaging short-form videos
- Write captions and prepare posts
- Schedule and distribute content across your connected social channels

A single recording typically becomes 10 to 15 pieces of content.

## Your social accounts

To publish for you, we need access to your social channels. We coordinate that with you through each platform's own access tools. You never share a password.

## Your next session

On the monthly plan, book your next session from your **Start Here** guide in Whop. On the weekly plan, your sessions recur at the same day and time.

Your content session is a 60-minute recorded conversation. You don't need a script. We guide the conversation and help you share your expertise naturally.

## Set up your space

We record remotely in Riverside, and we send you the link before your session. You don't need a studio. A few small adjustments make a big difference, and we check your framing and lighting with you at the start of the session before we begin.

<Note>
  Don't let setup hold you back. Content that gives real value works even when the camera angle isn't perfect.
</Note>

### Camera

- A good webcam, your phone, or a camera connected to your computer all work.
- Raise the camera to about eye level. Looking down slightly is fine, but a camera that sits well below your face is not flattering.
- Keep the camera level and square to the room. If the wall lines behind you run at an angle, straighten the camera until they look parallel to the edge of the frame.
- Frame yourself using the rule of thirds: your eyes should sit about one third of the way down from the top of the frame.

### Lighting

- Put light on your face. A small key light or ring light in front of you helps a lot.
- No light handy? Set a spare monitor to a plain, bright white screen and use it as a soft light.
- Watch for a bright hot spot on your head from a light directly above you. Dim that light, shift your position, or soften it with a diffuser.

### On camera

- Look straight into the camera when you answer, and speak with confidence.
- We ask the questions, so you can focus on answering one at a time. Expect a mix of deeper questions and quicker, rapid-fire ones.

## What we make from your session

Going deep on one topic for the hour gives us the most to work with. From a single recording we typically cut:

- One long-form video
- Two or three medium videos, three to ten minutes each, that each answer one specific question
- About a dozen shorts, 15 to 90 seconds each

## What you don't need to do

- Write a script
- Edit, caption, or schedule anything

Come ready to talk about what you know. That's the part only you can do.

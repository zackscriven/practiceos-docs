Your content session is a 60-minute recorded conversation. You don't need a script. We guide the conversation and help you share your expertise naturally.

## Before your first session

<Steps>
  <Step title="Complete your content onboarding">
    Fill out the onboarding form linked from your **Start Here** guide in Whop. It takes about 3 to 5 minutes and asks about your business, your ideal audience, and what you'd like to accomplish.

    We use your answers, along with your website and public social media profiles, to prepare your content strategy, so complete it before you record.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Book your session">
    [Book your content session](https://links.privatepracticeos.app/widget/booking/oy6tQhe4mBpy6NSwzOwW). It's a 60-minute recording with Zack Scriven. On the monthly plan you book each session individually. On the weekly plan you record at a consistent day and time each week.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Know which social accounts you want to publish to">
    We publish to your connected social channels. We'll help you grant access through each platform's own tools.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Please don't send us your passwords. Account access is always granted through the platform itself.
</Warning>

## What you don't need to do

- Write a script
- Edit, caption, or schedule anything

Come ready to talk about what you know. That's the part only you can do.

## Rescheduling

Need to move your session? Contact us at least 24 hours before your recording. Rescheduling is subject to availability.

Use **Support** in Whop for scheduling questions. See [Get help](/support).

Work through these in order. Each step links to the full guide. If you're on the Practice Growth System, your onboarding call covers these steps with you — use this page to prepare beforehand and as a reference afterward.

## Before you start

Have these logins at hand. Setup goes much faster when you don't have to go looking for them.

- Your **domain registrar** — wherever your website's domain is managed
- The **Google account** you use for business email and calendar
- Your **Facebook** login, and whether your business already has a Meta business portfolio and ad account
- Your **LinkedIn** login — the one connected to your company page
- Your **YouTube** login, if you have a channel
- What you use today for business **calls and texts**

## Setup steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in and secure your account">
    Change your temporary password and turn on two-factor authentication.
    [Sign in to PracticeOS →](/setup/sign-in)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Understand your wallet">
    Your account has a usage wallet that's separate from your subscription.
    [Billing and your wallet →](/administration/billing)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect your email and calendar">
    Connect email first; your calendar connects with it.
    [Connect email →](/integrations/email)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set your availability">
    Timezone, working hours, and the calendars that block your time.
    [Availability →](/calendar/availability)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect your domain">
    Add the subdomain your pages and forms publish on. Your existing website isn't changed.
    [Connect your domain →](/website-growth/website)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set up your sending domain">
    So automated emails send from your own domain and reach the inbox.
    [Email sending →](/communications/email-sending)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add tracking">
    Google Tag Manager and your Meta Pixel, so you can see which campaigns bring inquiries.
    [Tracking and marketing integrations →](/website-growth/marketing-integrations)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect your social and ad accounts">
    Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, and YouTube.
    [Meta / Facebook →](/integrations/meta-facebook)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set up your texting number">
    A local number for automated texts, and the registration it needs before texting goes live.
    [Phone and SMS →](/communications/phone-sms)
  </Step>
</Steps>

## 1. Search these docs

Press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> / <kbd>⌘</kbd> + <kbd>K</kbd> and search for the screen or task you're stuck on.

## 2. Contact us through Whop

Message the Private Practice Collective team through the Whop app — the same account you use to manage your PracticeOS subscription.
{/* VERIFY: exact place in Whop to message support (DM, support chat, or a named channel) and its label — confirm in the live Whop hub before approving. */}

Include:

- What you were trying to do
- Which page or screen you were on
- What happened instead, and any error message — a screenshot helps

<Warning>
Don't include client names, contact details, or anything about a client's care in support messages or screenshots. Crop or blur them out first — we never need client information to help with a setup question.
</Warning>

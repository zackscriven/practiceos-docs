Connect your email first, then your calendar. They're two separate connections, both made from your own settings.

## Before you begin

- Signed in to PracticeOS
- The login for the Google or Microsoft account you use for practice email and calendar

## Connect your email

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your email settings">
    Go to **Settings → My Profile → Email and conversations**. Under **Email (2-way sync)**, start the connection.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your account">
    Pick the account you use for the practice and approve the permissions it asks for. When it's done, the account shows as **Connected**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Approve a second time if asked">
    The permission prompt can appear twice. That's expected — the first pass grants your browser permission and the second completes the link.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
Two-way sync pulls in email history only for people who are **already contacts** in PracticeOS, and only from the day you connect. It does not import your whole inbox.
</Note>

## Connect your calendar

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open calendar connections">
    Go to **Settings → Calendars → Connections**. Under **Connected calendars**, click **+ Add new**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in to your calendar account">
    Choose the same account and approve access.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Keep event details private">
    Leave **Private mode for synced events → Hide event details** turned on. Your team then sees that you're busy, not what your personal events are.
  </Step>
</Steps>

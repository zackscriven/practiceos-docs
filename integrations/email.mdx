Connect your business email first. When you connect a Google account, your calendar connects along with it.

## Before you begin

- Signed in to PracticeOS
- The login for the Google account you use for practice email and calendar

## Connect

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your user settings">
    Email is connected per user, from your own profile settings.
    {/* VERIFY: exact path to the per-user email/calendar connection screen and the button label. */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your Google account">
    Pick the account you use for the practice and approve the permissions it asks for.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Approve a second time if asked">
    The connection prompt can appear twice. That's expected — the first pass grants your browser permission and the second completes the link.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What syncs, and what doesn't

<Note>
Two-way sync pulls in email history only for people who are **already contacts** in PracticeOS, and only from the day you connect. It does not import your whole inbox.
</Note>

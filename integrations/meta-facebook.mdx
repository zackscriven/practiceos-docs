## 1. Sort out ownership in Meta first

Do this in Meta Business Suite before connecting anything in PracticeOS.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Give your login full access to your business portfolio">
    The Facebook login you'll connect with needs full access to your business portfolio.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Claim your ad account">
    Claim your ad account into the portfolio using its ad account ID.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## 2. Connect in PracticeOS

Go to **Settings → Integrations** and click **Connect** on the **Facebook** card, then on the **Instagram** card. The Facebook connection brings in ad leads, messages, and reviews from your page.

If you don't have an Instagram business account yet, create a real one. An active organic presence helps your ads.

## Two places to connect

Accounts connect in two separate places, and you need both:

- **Settings → Integrations** — syncs messages and leads into PracticeOS
- **Social Planner** — used for scheduling and publishing posts

## LinkedIn and YouTube

Connect LinkedIn from its card in **Settings → Integrations**, using the login that manages your company page — the company page appears through your personal profile, so check you're signed in to the right account before connecting. Connect YouTube if you plan to publish longer videos.

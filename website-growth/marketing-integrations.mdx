## How tracking fits together

| Layer | What it tells you |
| --- | --- |
| **Pixel** | Someone viewed a page |
| **Conversion events** | Someone took an action, such as submitting a form — sent to the ad platform |
| **Campaign parameters (UTMs)** | Which campaign and ad the inquiry came from — recorded in PracticeOS |

## Google Tag Manager and the Meta Pixel

<Steps>
  <Step title="One container per domain">
    Use one Google Tag Manager container for each domain.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the Meta Pixel">
    Add the Meta Pixel from the Tag Manager template gallery and set it to fire on **All Pages**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify it fires">
    Use a pixel-helper browser extension on your pages to confirm the pixel is firing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the container to your PracticeOS pages">
    Pages on your `pages.` subdomain don't inherit tags from your main website. Add the container code to them separately.

</Steps>

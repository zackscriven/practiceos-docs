## See your appointments

Click **Calendars** in the left menu. Two views are available at the top:

- **Calendar view** — appointments laid out by day or week
- **Appointment list view** — the same appointments as a list, handy for searching

## Booking calendars

A **booking calendar** is what people book into — for example, a free consultation or a first session. Each one has its own length, availability, and booking page.

Booking calendars are created and managed in **Settings → Calendars → Calendars** with **+ New calendar**. The same screen has tabs for a **Service menu**, **Rooms**, and **Equipment** if your practice books those.

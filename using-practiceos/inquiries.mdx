Every new inquiry can be tracked as an **opportunity** in a **pipeline**. The pipeline is a board of columns, one per stage, so you can see at a glance who's waiting on a reply, who's been contacted, and who's booked.

## Open your pipeline

1. Click **Opportunities** in the left menu.
2. Choose your pipeline from the dropdown at the top left. Each card on the board is one inquiry.
3. Switch between the board and a list using the view icons at the top right.

Your stage names are set up for your practice and may differ from other accounts.

## Move an inquiry forward

When an inquiry moves on — you've replied, or they've booked — move its card to the next stage.

## Add an inquiry by hand

For someone who called or emailed you directly, click **Add opportunity** and fill in their details, so they're tracked like everyone else.

## Change your stages

Pipelines and their stages are edited in **Settings → Opportunities & Pipelines**. Talk to your Private Practice Collective contact before changing stages — your automations may depend on them.

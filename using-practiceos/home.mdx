Everything in PracticeOS starts from the menu on the left. The top of the menu shows which practice account you're in.

## Everyday work

| Menu item | Use it to |
| --- | --- |
| **Launchpad** | Work through your account setup checklist |
| **Dashboard** | See an overview of your account |
| **Conversations** | Read and reply to texts, emails, and messages in one inbox — see [Your inbox](/using-practiceos/inbox) |
| **Calendars** | See booked appointments by calendar or as a list — see [Your calendar](/using-practiceos/calendar) |
| **Contacts** | Look up and organize the people who have reached out — see [Contacts](/using-practiceos/contacts) |
| **Opportunities** | Track each new inquiry from first contact to booked — see [Track inquiries](/using-practiceos/inquiries) |
| **Payments** | Payments and invoices |

## Growing your practice

| Menu item | Use it to |
| --- | --- |
| **Marketing** | Email, social posting, and other outreach |
| **Automation** | The workflows that send follow-ups and reminders for you |
| **Sites** | Your landing pages and forms |
| **Reputation** | Reviews |
| **Reporting** | Results over time |

**Settings**, at the bottom of the menu, is where account setup lives: your profile, calendars, email, phone, domains, and integrations.

<Tip>
Press <kbd>⌘</kbd>/<kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>K</kbd> inside PracticeOS to search your account.
</Tip>

**Contacts** holds everyone who has reached out to your practice or booked with you.

## The Contacts tabs

| Tab | What it's for |
| --- | --- |
| **Smart Lists** | Saved, filtered views of your contacts |
| **Bulk Actions** | Changes made to many contacts at once, and their history |
| **Custom Fields** | Extra details your practice records for each contact |
| **Tasks** | Follow-up tasks assigned to you and your team |
| **Companies** | Organizations your contacts belong to, such as referral sources |

## Import an existing list

To bring in contacts from another system, use **Settings → Import Data**.

<Warning>
Only import what you need to schedule and follow up. Don't import clinical notes, diagnoses, or session details.
</Warning>

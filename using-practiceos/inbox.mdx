**Conversations** brings your texts, emails, and connected social messages into one inbox, so you don't have to check each app separately.

## Find the messages that need you

Open **Conversations**. The inbox has four views:

- **Unread** — new messages you haven't opened. This is where to start each day.
- **All** — every conversation.
- **Recent** — conversations with the latest activity.
- **Starred** — conversations you've flagged to come back to.

Select a conversation to see the full thread. The contact's details appear on the right.

## Reply

Type your reply at the bottom of the conversation and send it. Choose the channel — text or email — before sending.

## Save time with snippets

The **Snippets** tab at the top of Conversations holds saved replies for messages you send often — directions to your office, what to expect at a first session, how to reschedule. Insert one instead of retyping it.

<Warning>
Keep clinical detail out of texts and emails. Use messages for scheduling and practical follow-up only.
</Warning>

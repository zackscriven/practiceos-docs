The Growth System brings your marketing, client acquisition, communications, and business systems together in one place. Work through these steps in order. You don't have to finish them all in one sitting.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Schedule your 1:1 onboarding">
    Book your session from the **Start Here** guide in Whop. On the call we activate your account, connect your accounts, and get you comfortable with the platform.

    See [Prepare for your 1:1 onboarding](/growth-system/onboarding-call) for what to have ready.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Activate your PracticeOS account">
    Once your account is ready, your login information arrives by email. Sign in at [my.privatepracticeos.app](https://my.privatepracticeos.app), change your password, and turn on two-factor authentication.

    See [Set up your account](/growth-system/account-setup).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set up your usage wallet">
    Add a payment card under **Settings → Billing** for usage-based services such as texting, email sending, and phone numbers.

    See [Usage wallet](/growth-system/account-setup#set-up-your-usage-wallet).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect your email and calendar">
    Sync your business email and calendar so conversations and appointments show up in PracticeOS.

    See [Connect your email and calendar](/growth-system/email-calendar).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect your social accounts and website">
    Connect your business social profiles so you can schedule posts, and add your website so page views and form submissions flow into PracticeOS.

    See [Connect your social accounts](/growth-system/social-accounts) and [Connect your website](/growth-system/website).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Join your weekly growth calls and community">
    Your membership includes weekly group growth calls and a private clients-only Discord community.

    See [Weekly calls and community](/growth-system/calls-community).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Checklist

- Schedule your 1:1 onboarding
- Activate your PracticeOS account
- Turn on two-factor authentication
- Review your Launchpad
- Set up your usage wallet
- Connect your email and calendar
- Access your private Discord community
- Find your next weekly growth call

## Want us to create your content too?

The [Content Engine](/content-engine/quickstart) is a separate, optional membership. You record with us for an hour and we turn that conversation into content for your social channels. Your Growth System membership stays exactly as it is.

There are two parts to connecting your website. You can do them in either order.

## Set up a landing-page subdomain

Landing pages and funnels built in PracticeOS live on a subdomain of your own domain, for example `pages.yourdomain.com`. A funnel works best for one specific offer to one specific audience, such as a lead magnet or an application to work with you, and it has conversion tracking built in.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose a subdomain">
    We pick this with you on your onboarding call. `pages` is a common choice.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a CNAME record">
    Sign in to your domain registrar or DNS provider and add a **CNAME** record. Use your subdomain (for example `pages`) as the host, and point it to the value we give you on the call.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Let us know">
    Tell us when the record is saved so we can verify it. DNS changes can take a few minutes to appear.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Your main website stays exactly where it is. Only the new subdomain points to PracticeOS.

## Add the tracking script to your existing website

We give you a short script during onboarding. Once it's on your site, PracticeOS records:

- **Page views**, so you can see which contacts visited your site.
- **Form submissions**, so someone who fills out a form on your existing website shows up in PracticeOS automatically.

### Add it directly

Paste the script on every page of your site, just before the closing `</body>` tag. Most website builders have a site-wide "footer code" or "custom code" setting for this.

### Add it with Google Tag Manager

If your site already uses Google Tag Manager, create a new **Custom HTML** tag, paste the script in, and set it to fire on all pages. Tag Manager becomes more useful as you add more tags later, such as a Meta Pixel.

<Note>
  The script works with standard HTML forms. If your forms are embedded in an iframe from another tool, they may need a different setup. [Ask us](/support) and we'll take a look.
</Note>

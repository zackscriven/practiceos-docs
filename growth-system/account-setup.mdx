## Sign in

Once your account is ready, you receive your login information by email.

1. Go to [my.privatepracticeos.app](https://my.privatepracticeos.app).
2. Sign in using your temporary password.
3. Change your password and turn on two-factor authentication (2FA).
4. Open **Launchpad** to review your setup checklist.

We walk through the important steps during your [1:1 onboarding](/growth-system/onboarding-call).

## Complete your business profile

In **Settings**, open your business profile and:

- Upload your full logo and a sidebar icon.
- Enter your business information.
- Check that your time zone is correct.

Save your changes when you're done.

<Tip>
  We recommend not allowing duplicate contacts. With duplicates turned off, each contact is matched on a key value such as email, then phone, which keeps your contact list clean.
</Tip>

## Set up your usage wallet

Your Growth System subscription is managed through Whop. Some services have additional usage costs, including:

- SMS and email sending
- Business phone numbers and calling
- Additional domains

To add a payment card, go to **Settings → Billing**.

<Note>
  Your advertising budget is separate. You pay it directly to platforms like Meta or Google.
</Note>

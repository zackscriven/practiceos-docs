## Weekly growth calls

Your membership includes weekly group growth calls where we work on building, growing, and scaling your practice. Bring your questions, challenges, and wins.

Topics include:

- Client acquisition and lead generation
- Marketing campaigns and automations
- Sales pipelines and follow-up
- Improving your systems and workflows
- Sustainable practice growth

Find the schedule under **Weekly Calls** in Whop. These calls are included in your membership and are separate from your 1:1 onboarding.

## Private client community

Your membership includes access to our private clients-only Discord community, granted through Whop. Use it to connect with fellow practice owners, exchange ideas, ask questions, and celebrate wins.

<Warning>
  Never share client health information in Discord.
</Warning>

For account, billing, or technical questions, use **Support** in Whop instead. See [Get help](/support).

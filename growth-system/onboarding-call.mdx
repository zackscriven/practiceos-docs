Your 1:1 onboarding is a working session. We share screens, activate your account, and connect as much as we can while you're on the call. It is separate from your weekly group growth calls.

<Card title="Book your 1:1 onboarding call" icon="calendar" href="https://links.privatepracticeos.app/widget/booking/hGtTMjeeu2mF9Bz54xqu" horizontal>
  Pick a time that works for you.
</Card>

## Fill out the onboarding form

Before your call, [fill out the onboarding form](https://links.privatepracticeos.app/widget/form/LaAMeIB7Jq8Vaz9d9W37) and choose **Growth System** as your membership. It covers your business, your social channels, and your current setup (domain, website, email, and tools), so we can spend the call connecting things instead of collecting details.

## Have access to these before the call

- Your website domain registrar (where your domain's DNS is managed)
- Your Google or Microsoft business email
- Your business calendar
- Your Facebook and Meta business accounts
- LinkedIn and your company page
- YouTube, if you use it
- Your current business phone service

Don't worry if you don't have everything ready. We'll help you prioritize.

<Warning>
  Never send us your passwords. You connect each account yourself, securely, through that provider's own sign-in.
</Warning>

## What we cover on the call

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in and account settings">
    We add you as an admin, you sign in, and we review your business profile: logo, business information, and time zone. See [Set up your account](/growth-system/account-setup).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Your landing-page subdomain">
    We set up a subdomain on your domain, such as `pages.yourdomain.com`, for landing pages and funnels. You add one DNS record at your registrar during the call. See [Connect your website](/growth-system/website).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Website tracking">
    We give you a short script to add to your existing website so page views and form submissions show up in PracticeOS.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Social accounts">
    We show you where to connect your business LinkedIn, Instagram, and Facebook so you can schedule content from one place. See [Connect your social accounts](/growth-system/social-accounts).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Email and calendar">
    We show you where to connect two-way email sync and your calendar. See [Connect your email and calendar](/growth-system/email-calendar).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Community and weekly calls">
    We invite you to the private client Discord and add you to the weekly growth calls.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## After the call

Sign in and finish connecting whatever we didn't get to. Two items most often carry over:

- Adding the tracking script to your website
- Creating or connecting your Meta business account, Facebook page, and Instagram profile

If you get stuck, [book a support call](https://links.privatepracticeos.app/widget/booking/lQt2Gsbnt47L3fcfA6ox) or see [Get help](/support).

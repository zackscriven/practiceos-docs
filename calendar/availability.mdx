## Before you begin

- Your [calendar is connected](/integrations/email#connect-your-calendar)

## Check your timezone

Every booking time is based on your timezone, so check it first.

- Your account's timezone is in **Settings → Business Profile → Time Zone**.
- Each availability schedule also has its own **Timezone** setting (next step). Make sure they match.

## Set your weekly hours

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your schedule">
    Go to **Settings → Calendars → Availability**. Your default schedule is called **Work Hours**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply it to your calendar">
    Under **Active on**, choose the calendar the schedule applies to. A schedule that isn't applied to any calendar has no effect.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set your hours">
    Under **Weekly working hours**, tick the days you work and set a start and end time for each. Use **+** to add a second block on the same day — for example, a morning and an afternoon with a break between. The copy icon next to a day copies its hours to other days.
    {/* VERIFY: what the copy icon offers (all days vs chosen days) — tooltip not observed. */}
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Block off specific dates

For a day off or a one-time change, don't edit your weekly hours. Use **Date specific hours → + Add date specific hours** to mark yourself unavailable (or available) on particular dates.

## Block time with your own calendar

Events on your connected calendar can block booking times too. If you keep a personal calendar, connect it and use it to block time — your personal events then make those times unavailable without their details appearing in PracticeOS.

<Tip>
Changing your weekly hours to block one afternoon is easy to forget to undo. A date-specific change or a calendar event clears itself once it's passed.
</Tip>

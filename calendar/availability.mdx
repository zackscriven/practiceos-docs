## Block time with a conflict calendar

To block time off, add it to your **conflict calendar** rather than changing your working hours. Anything on a conflict calendar makes that time unavailable for booking.

If you keep a separate personal calendar, subscribe it as a busy-only conflict calendar. Your personal events then block booking times without their details appearing in PracticeOS.

<Tip>
Changing your working hours to block one afternoon is easy to forget to undo. A conflict-calendar event clears itself once it's passed.
</Tip>

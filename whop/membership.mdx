PracticeOS memberships are sold and managed through Whop. When you join the Growth System or the Content Engine, Whop is where your subscription, guides, and community access live.

<Card title="Private Practice Collective on Whop" icon="arrow-up-right" href="https://whop.com/privatepracticecollective/" horizontal>
  whop.com/privatepracticecollective
</Card>

## What you'll find in Whop

- **Start Here guide** for your membership, with your first steps
- **Weekly Calls**, the schedule for the group growth calls (Growth System)
- **Discord access** to the private clients-only community
- **Support**, in the left-hand menu, for account, billing, scheduling, or technical questions
- **Your subscription**, where you manage billing for your membership

## What is billed where

| What | Where it's billed |
| --- | --- |
| Growth System or Content Engine membership | Whop |
| Usage inside PracticeOS, such as texting, email sending, and phone numbers | Your PracticeOS usage wallet. See [Set up your account](/growth-system/account-setup#set-up-your-usage-wallet) |
| Advertising budget | Paid directly to the ad platform |

## Add or change a membership

Our memberships are on our [Whop page](https://whop.com/privatepracticecollective/). The Growth System and the Content Engine are separate subscriptions, so adding one leaves the other exactly as it is.

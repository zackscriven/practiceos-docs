## How it fits with the Growth System

The two work together. The Growth System is where your leads, conversations, follow-up, and booking live. Whop adds a place to sell and another way to advertise, which can open up channels such as TikTok or LinkedIn. Used together, each makes the other more valuable.

Your advertising budget is always separate from your membership and is paid to the ad platform.

## How to start

1. Sign up for Whop with [our partner link](https://whop.com/start/?code=PRIVATEPRACTICECOLLECTIVE).
2. Tell us you've joined: use **Support** in Whop or mention it on a weekly growth call.
3. We schedule your Whop onboarding and plan your ads launch with you.

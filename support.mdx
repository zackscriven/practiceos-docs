## Contact support

For account, billing, scheduling, or technical questions, use **Support** in the left-hand menu in Whop.

Tell us:

- What you were trying to do
- What happened
- Which screen you were using

Screenshots help. Before you send one, blur or remove client names, contact information, and sensitive clinical details.

<Warning>
  Never share client health information in Discord or in general support conversations.
</Warning>

## Book a support call

If you'd rather work through it live, [book a support call](https://links.privatepracticeos.app/widget/booking/lQt2Gsbnt47L3fcfA6ox). Tell us what you need help with when you book so we can come prepared.

## Other places to get answers

- **Weekly growth calls** (Growth System members): bring questions and work through them live. See [Weekly calls and community](/growth-system/calls-community).
- **Private client Discord**: ask fellow members and share what's working.
- **These docs**: use search, or ask the assistant at the top of any page.

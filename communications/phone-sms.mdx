Your current business phone stays exactly as it is. PracticeOS adds a separate **local number** used for automated texting — the first reply to a new inquiry, appointment reminders, and follow-ups.

## Cost

Phone numbers and texts are usage charges paid from your [wallet](/administration/billing).

## Register your number before texting goes live

US carriers require business texting numbers to be registered before they'll deliver automated texts. This registration is called **A2P 10DLC**.

- Registration can take more than one attempt. A rejection usually means a detail needs correcting, not that you can't register.
- A **contact page on your own domain** helps your registration pass.

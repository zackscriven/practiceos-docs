Automated emails — confirmations, reminders, follow-ups — send from a dedicated subdomain such as `lc.yourpractice.com`. Replies still go to your real inbox.

## Before you begin

- Your [domain is connected](/website-growth/website), or you have your registrar login ready

## Set it up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add the sending subdomain">
    Go to **Settings → Email Services → SMTP Service** and click **Dedicated Domain And IP**. Add `lc.yourpractice.com` as your sending domain. PracticeOS shows you the DNS records to create.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create SPF, DKIM, and DMARC records">
    Add each record at your registrar exactly as shown.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check every record after saving">
    Long records sometimes get cut off when pasted. Open each one after saving and compare it character for character.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set your reply-to address">
    In **Settings → Email Services → Reply & Forward Settings**, set replies to go to the inbox you actually read.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
DMARC isn't optional. Major inbox providers reject or junk mail from domains without it.
</Warning>

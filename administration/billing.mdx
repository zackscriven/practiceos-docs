Your PracticeOS account has two separate kinds of billing.

| | What it covers | How it's paid |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Subscription** | Your PracticeOS plan | Billed separately — never taken from your wallet |
| **Wallet** | Usage: text messages, email sends, phone numbers, AI features, additional domains | A card on file; charged at cost |

## How the wallet works

The wallet is pre-loaded with a small balance and tops itself up from your card as you use it. Usage is passed through at cost — there's no markup on texts, emails, numbers, or AI usage.

## See your usage charges

Your card on file and wallet are managed in **Settings → Billing**, on the **Billing Dashboard**. A payment method is required before the wallet can be used.
